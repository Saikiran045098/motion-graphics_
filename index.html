FAQ

Good questions, answered.

Do I need to write the script?

No. I write it from our discovery call and your existing docs. You approve it before any animation starts.

Our UI changes every sprint. Will the video go stale?

I rebuild key screens in Figma and animate them, so small UI changes don't break the video. Updates to existing videos are billed at a reduced rate.

Can you match our brand guidelines?

Yes. Send your brand kit and I'll build a motion template that keeps every future video on brand.

How does payment work?

50% to start and 50% on final delivery.

Do you give discounts for multiple videos?

Yes. If you book more than 2 projects, the price is negotiable. Message me on WhatsApp or by email with what you need.

Who owns the final video?

You do. Full commercial rights transfer on final payment, and stock music and voice licenses are included.
